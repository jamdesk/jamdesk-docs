## Automatic Builds

Jamdesk starts your first build as soon as you connect a repository. Every later push to your connected branch triggers a new build.

## Build Progress

Track your build in the dashboard:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Queued">
    Your build is waiting to start. Peak times can add a brief queue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cloning">
    Jamdesk pulls your repository content from GitHub.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Validating">
    Jamdesk checks your `docs.json` and MDX files for errors.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Building">
    Jamdesk compiles MDX to HTML with syntax highlighting and a search index.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deploying">
    Built files upload to the global CDN.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Live">
    Your docs are available at your project URL.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Viewing Your Live Docs

Click **View Site** in the dashboard or visit `https://your-project.jamdesk.app`.

Your docs also appear on your custom domain, if you configured one.

## Manual Rebuild

Rebuild without pushing changes:

1. Go to your project in the [dashboard](https://dashboard.jamdesk.com)
2. Open **Settings**
3. Click **Rebuild**

Use this after changing environment variables or to force a fresh build.

## If Your Build Fails

The dashboard shows an error message for a failed build. Common causes:

- Invalid `docs.json` syntax
- MDX syntax errors
- Missing pages referenced in navigation
