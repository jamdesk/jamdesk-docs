Invite colleagues to monitor builds, review analytics, and trigger rebuilds. Roles are Owner and Member.

<Note>
Membership also decides whose GitHub pushes build. Jamdesk matches the person who pushed by their linked GitHub account or by their email. A push from a non-member fails the build. The project owner gets an email. Invite the pusher with their commit email. They must sign in to Jamdesk with that email to accept. If they're already a member, they can [link their GitHub account](/integrations/github#personal-github-account-user-level) instead.
</Note>

## Inviting Members

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open project settings">
    Go to your project and click **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Members">
    Navigate to the **Members** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter their email">
    Type the email address in the **Invite Member** field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invitation">
    Click **Invite**. They'll receive an email with a link to join.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Invitees need a Jamdesk account. The invitation prompts them to create one if they have none.
</Note>

<Tip>
If your account has [Single Sign-On](/setup/sso) set up, teammates whose email matches your verified domain sign in through your identity provider instead of creating a password. The invite form flags when an address will use SSO.
</Tip>

## Member Roles

| Role | Permissions |
|------|-------------|
| **Owner** | Full access, can remove members, transfer ownership, delete project |
| **Member** | View builds and analytics, trigger rebuilds |

Click **Transfer Ownership** next to a member's name to make them the owner. See [Ownership Transfer](/help/projects/ownership-transfer) for details.

<Note>
**Team members are not the same as docs-site readers.** Team members see builds and analytics in the Jamdesk dashboard. [Access Control](/setup/access-control) sets who can _read_ your published docs site: public, password-gated, mixed, or SSO.
</Note>

## Removing Members

Only the project owner can remove team members:

1. Go to **Settings** → **Members**
2. Find the member
3. Click **Remove**
4. Confirm the removal

Removed members lose access immediately. You can re-invite them later.

## Pending Invitations

Invitations that haven't been accepted yet appear in the **Members** list, marked as pending. Cancel one with the trash icon next to it.

Pending invitations don't expire. They stay valid until the person accepts or you cancel them.

## Team size

Team members are unlimited on every project, at no extra charge.
