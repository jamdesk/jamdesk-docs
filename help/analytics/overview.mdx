This overview explains the analytics dashboard in the Jamdesk Help Center and what each metric means. Use it to understand page views, visitors, and engagement before diving into troubleshooting or setup details. It also points you to related analytics setup and troubleshooting guides so you can dig deeper. Use it as a baseline before you interpret trends or report on performance.

## Accessing Analytics

1. Select your project from the dashboard
2. Click **Analytics** in the sidebar

## Dashboard Overview

The analytics dashboard shows:

- **Page views** — Total pages viewed
- **Unique visitors** — Individual users
- **Average time on page** — Engagement metric
- **Top pages** — Most visited documentation

## Time Range

Select the time period to analyze:

- Last 24 hours
- Last 7 days
- Last 30 days
- Custom range

## Page Views

Track which pages users visit most:

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Views | Total times the page was loaded |
| Unique views | Individual users who viewed the page |
| Avg. time | Average time spent on the page |
| Bounce rate | Users who left without visiting another page |

### Top Pages Report

See your most popular documentation:

1. Go to **Analytics** → **Pages**
2. Sort by views, time, or bounce rate
3. Click a page to see detailed metrics

## Visitors

Understand your audience:

- **New vs returning** — First-time vs repeat visitors
- **Geography** — Visitor locations by country
- **Devices** — Desktop, mobile, tablet breakdown
- **Browsers** — Chrome, Safari, Firefox, etc.

## Traffic Sources

See where visitors come from:

- **Direct** — Typed URL or bookmark
- **Search** — Google, Bing, etc.
- **Referral** — Links from other sites
- **Social** — Twitter, LinkedIn, etc.

## Data Retention

Analytics data is retained for:

| Plan | Retention |
|------|-----------|
| Trial | 7 days |
| Pro | 90 days |

## Privacy

Jamdesk analytics:

- Don't use cookies
- Don't track individual users
- Are GDPR compliant
- Respect Do Not Track
