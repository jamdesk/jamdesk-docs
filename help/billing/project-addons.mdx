Need more than 3 projects? Add extra project slots to your Pro plan.

## How Add-ons Work

Pro plans include 3 projects. Additional projects are $15/month each.

## Adding Projects

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Billing">
    Navigate to **Settings** → **Billing**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add Projects">
    Select **Add project slots**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose quantity">
    Select how many additional projects you need.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Review the updated total and confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Add-ons are prorated—you're only charged for the remaining days in your billing cycle.

## Removing Projects

To reduce your project count:

1. Delete projects until you're at your desired count
2. Go to **Settings** → **Billing**
3. Click **Manage add-ons**
4. Reduce the number of extra slots
5. Confirm the change

<Warning>
You can't reduce below the number of active projects. Delete projects first.
</Warning>

## Billing for Add-ons

- Add-ons appear as a separate line item on invoices
- Prorated charges apply when adding mid-cycle
- Credits apply when removing mid-cycle
