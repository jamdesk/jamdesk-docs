Jamdesk offers simple, transparent pricing with a 14-day free trial.

## How the Free Trial Works

When you sign up, you enter your payment method and start a 14-day free trial with full access to all Pro features. You won't be charged until the trial ends.

### What Happens During the Trial

- You get full access to all Pro features from day one
- Your payment method is saved but not charged
- You can cancel anytime before the trial ends at no cost
- We send you a reminder email **3 days before** your trial ends

### When the Trial Ends

If you keep your subscription, your payment method is automatically charged and your Pro plan begins. Nothing changes — your projects, settings, and team members stay exactly as they are.

If you cancel before the trial ends, you're never charged.

### After Cancellation

If your subscription is canceled (either during trial or later):

- Your existing docs remain accessible to visitors
- Builds are disabled
- Your project becomes read-only in the dashboard
- You can resubscribe anytime to restore full access

## Pricing FAQ

<Accordion title="Is there a free plan?">
  We offer a 14-day free trial with full Pro features. After that, the Pro plan is $29/month per project. We don't have a permanent free tier, but you can cancel during the trial at no cost.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I pay annually?">
  Yes! Annual billing saves 20%. Contact support to switch to annual billing.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens if I cancel?">
  Your docs remain accessible until the end of your billing period. After that, the project becomes read-only.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do you offer discounts?">
  We offer discounts for:
  - Open source projects (50% off)
  - Startups (varies)
  - Non-profits (50% off)

  Contact support to apply.
</Accordion>
