There's one paid plan, and you can use all of it before you pay anything.

## How the Free Trial Works

The trial starts when you create your account and lasts 14 days. You don't enter a card to sign up, and every Pro feature is on from day one.

### What Happens During the Trial

- Add a card from the dashboard at any point. We charge it when the trial ends, not before.
- To start your paid plan early, add a card, then select **Start Paid Plan Now** on the Subscription page. Do this to add more projects or skip the rest of the trial.
- Until you add a card, we email you on day 7, on day 10, and when the trial ends.

### When the Trial Ends

If you added a card, we charge it and your Pro plan begins. Your projects, settings, and team members stay as they are.

If you didn't add a card, your projects pause on day 14:

- Sites on a `*.jamdesk.app` subdomain show a "This site is currently paused" page
- Sites on a custom domain stay live and keep serving their last published build
- Builds stop and your projects become read-only in the dashboard

Subscribe to bring your sites and builds back. The trial is already used, so you're charged right away.

### After Cancellation

A canceled subscription works the same way as a trial that ended without a card:

- Sites on a `*.jamdesk.app` subdomain show the paused page
- Sites on a custom domain keep serving their last published build
- Builds stop and your projects become read-only in the dashboard
- Resubscribe anytime to restore full access

## Pricing FAQ

<Accordion title="Is there a free plan?">
  There's no permanent free tier. The 14-day free trial includes every Pro feature and doesn't need a credit card. After that, the Pro plan is $29/month and includes one docs project.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do I need a credit card to start the trial?">
  No. Sign up and the trial starts right away. Add a card before day 14 to keep your sites running without a break.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I pay annually?">
  Yes. Annual billing is $290/year, which is two months free. Additional projects are $150/year each on annual billing. Contact support to switch to annual billing.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens if I cancel?">
  Your plan stays active until the end of your billing period. After that, `*.jamdesk.app` sites show a paused page, custom-domain sites keep serving their last build, and your projects become read-only.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do you offer discounts?">
  We offer discounts for:
  - Open source projects (50% off)
  - Startups (varies)
  - Non-profits (50% off)

  Contact support to apply.
</Accordion>
