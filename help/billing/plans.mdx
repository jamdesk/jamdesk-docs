Jamdesk offers simple, transparent pricing with a 14-day free trial.

## Plans

### Free Trial

Try Jamdesk free for 14 days:

- 1 project
- 2 team members
- Full feature access
- No credit card required

### Pro Plan

$29/month per project:

- Unlimited pages
- 10 team members
- 90-day analytics retention
- Custom domains
- Priority support
- Slack integration

## Feature Comparison

| Feature | Trial | Pro |
|---------|-------|-----|
| Projects | 1 | 3 (expandable) |
| Team members | 2 | 10 |
| Custom domains | ✓ | ✓ |
| Analytics retention | 7 days | 90 days |
| API access | ✓ | ✓ |
| Slack integration | - | ✓ |
| Support | Email | Priority |

## Trial Details

Your 14-day trial includes:

- Full access to all Pro features
- No credit card required to start
- Automatic reminder before expiry
- Easy upgrade to continue

### After Trial Ends

If you don't upgrade:

- Your project becomes read-only
- Existing docs remain accessible
- Builds are disabled
- You can upgrade anytime to restore access

## Pricing FAQ

<Accordion title="Is there a free plan?">
  We offer a 14-day free trial. After that, the Pro plan is $29/month per project. We don't have a permanent free tier.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I pay annually?">
  Yes! Annual billing saves 20%. Contact support to switch to annual billing.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens if I cancel?">
  Your docs remain accessible until the end of your billing period. After that, the project becomes read-only.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do you offer discounts?">
  We offer discounts for:
  - Open source projects (50% off)
  - Startups (varies)
  - Non-profits (50% off)

  Contact support to apply.
</Accordion>
