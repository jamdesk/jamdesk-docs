Jamdesk has one paid plan with a 14-day free trial.

## How the Free Trial Works

Enter your payment method at sign-up to start a 14-day free trial with every Pro feature. You won't be charged until the trial ends.

### What Happens During the Trial

- Every Pro feature is on from day one
- Your payment method is saved but not charged
- Cancel anytime before the trial ends at no cost
- **Start your paid plan early** to add more projects or skip the waiting period
- We send you a reminder email **3 days before** your trial ends

### When the Trial Ends

Your payment method is charged and your Pro plan begins. Your projects, settings, and team members stay as they are.

Cancel before the trial ends and you're never charged.

### After Cancellation

A canceled subscription, during the trial or later, has these effects:

- Your existing docs remain accessible to visitors
- Builds are disabled
- Your project becomes read-only in the dashboard
- Resubscribe anytime to restore full access

## Pricing FAQ

<Accordion title="Is there a free plan?">
  No permanent free tier. We offer a 14-day free trial with full Pro features. After that, the Pro plan is $29/month and includes one docs project. Cancel during the trial at no cost.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I pay annually?">
  Yes. Annual billing is $290/year, which is two months free. Additional projects are $150/year each on annual billing. Contact support to switch to annual billing.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens if I cancel?">
  Your docs remain accessible until the end of your billing period. After that, the project becomes read-only.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do you offer discounts?">
  We offer discounts for:
  - Open source projects (50% off)
  - Startups (varies)
  - Non-profits (50% off)

  Contact support to apply.
</Accordion>
