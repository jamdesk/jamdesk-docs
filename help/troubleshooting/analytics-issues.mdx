Most missing or unexpected analytics data comes from ad blockers, propagation delays, or data retention limits.

## No Data Showing

### Recently deployed

Analytics take a few hours to populate after your first deployment.

### Ad blockers

Some ad blockers prevent analytics from loading. Test with blockers disabled.

### Do Not Track

Jamdesk doesn't track visitors with Do Not Track enabled.

## Data Seems Wrong

### Page views too low

Possible causes:
- Ad blockers on visitor browsers
- Cached pages not triggering analytics
- Recent deployment (data still populating)

### Page views too high

Possible causes:
- Bot traffic (we filter most, but some gets through)
- Your own visits while testing
- Refreshing pages during development

<Tip>
Test in incognito mode to keep your own visits out of analytics.
</Tip>

## Search Analytics Empty

### Search not being used

Check whether visitors use search. Confirm the search box is easy to find and working.

### Recent deployment

Search analytics take 24-48 hours to populate after deployment.

### Indexing issues

Poor search results make visitors give up. Run test searches to confirm your content is indexed.

## Missing Pages in Reports

### Page not deployed

Verify the page exists in your live documentation.

### No traffic

Pages with zero views won't appear in reports.

### Data retention

| Plan | Retention |
|------|-----------|
| Trial | 7 days |
| Pro | 90 days |

Older data is automatically removed.

## Geographic Data Issues

### Location shows "Unknown"

Some visitors use VPNs or have location disabled.

### Wrong locations

IP geolocation is occasionally inaccurate. Large discrepancies may indicate a VPN.

## Exporting Data

To export analytics:

1. Go to **Analytics**
2. Select your date range
3. Click **Export**
4. Choose CSV or JSON format

## Real-Time Analytics

Jamdesk doesn't provide real-time analytics. Data updates every few hours.

Add a third-party analytics tool alongside Jamdesk for real-time data.
