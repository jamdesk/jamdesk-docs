Keep your account secure by using a strong, unique password.

## Changing Your Password

If you know your current password:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Settings">
    Click your avatar and select **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Security">
    Navigate to the **Security** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update password">
    Enter your current password, then your new password twice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Update Password** to apply the change.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Resetting a Forgotten Password

If you can't remember your password:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to login">
    Visit [app.jamdesk.com](https://app.jamdesk.com) and click **Sign In**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Forgot Password">
    Select the **Forgot password?** link below the sign-in form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your email">
    Enter the email address associated with your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your inbox">
    Click the reset link in the email we send you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set new password">
    Choose a new password and confirm it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Password reset links expire after 1 hour. Request a new one if yours has expired.
</Note>

## Password Requirements

Your password must:

- Be at least 8 characters long
- Not be a commonly used password
- Not match your email address

## OAuth Accounts

If you signed up with Google or GitHub, you don't have a Jamdesk password. To add one:

1. Go to **Settings** → **Security**
2. Click **Set Password**
3. Enter and confirm your new password

This lets you sign in with either method.
