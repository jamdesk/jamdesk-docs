Change your Jamdesk password, or reset it if you forget it.

<Note>
  This page covers your **Jamdesk account password** (how you sign in to the dashboard). To put a password on your published docs site, see [Password Protection](/setup/password-protection). See [Access Control](/setup/access-control) for an overview of public, password, and SSO options.
</Note>

## Changing Your Password

You need your current password for these steps.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to User Settings">
    Click your avatar in the sidebar and select **User Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Change Password">
    In the **Account Information** card, click **Change Password**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the form">
    A modal opens with three fields:

    1. **Current Password**
    2. **New Password**
    3. **Confirm New Password**

    Show or hide a password with the eye icon on its field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Click **Change Password** to save. A success message appears and the modal closes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
The Change Password button appears only for accounts created with email and password. Signed up with Google or GitHub? See [OAuth Accounts](#oauth-accounts) below.
</Note>

## Resetting a Forgotten Password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to login">
    Visit [dashboard.jamdesk.com](https://dashboard.jamdesk.com) and click **Sign In**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Forgot Password">
    Select the **Forgot password?** link below the password field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your email">
    Enter your account's email address. An address already typed on the sign-in form is pre-filled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your inbox">
    Click **Send reset email**. Open the password reset link in your inbox and follow the instructions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Password reset links expire after 1 hour. Request a new one if yours has expired.
</Note>

## Password Requirements

Your password must be at least **6 characters** long.

## OAuth Accounts

Accounts created only with Google or GitHub have no Jamdesk password. The **Change Password** button doesn't appear in their account settings.

Set a password for your email address with the **Forgot password?** flow on the login page. You can then sign in with a password or with OAuth.
