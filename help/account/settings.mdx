## Accessing Settings

1. Click your avatar in the top right corner
2. Select **Settings** from the dropdown

## Profile Information

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Profile">
    In Settings, select the **Profile** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update your information">
    Change your display name or email address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save changes">
    Click **Save** to apply your updates.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
A new email address needs verification. Open the confirmation link in your inbox.
</Note>

## Theme Preference

- **Light**: default light theme
- **Dark**: dark theme for low-light environments
- **System**: match your operating system preference

The theme setting applies to the Jamdesk dashboard. Set your documentation site theme in `docs.json`.

## Notification Preferences

Choose which emails you receive:

| Notification | Description |
|--------------|-------------|
| Build alerts | Email when builds fail |
| Team invites | Email when invited to a project |
| Product updates | Occasional product news and tips |
