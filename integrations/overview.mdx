This section covers three jobs.

**Documenting your API** means rendering OpenAPI specs into endpoint pages readers can try in the browser. **The Jamdesk Search API** turns your docs into a data source that chatbots, support tools, and AI agents can query. **Integrations** connect your docs site to other tools: build notifications in Slack, analytics, and email capture.
