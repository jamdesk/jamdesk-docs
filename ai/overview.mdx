AI tools asked about your product often work from stale training data or can't reach your docs at all. Every Jamdesk build generates three AI endpoints (`llms.txt`, an MCP server, and raw Markdown URLs). AI tools read your current docs from them without scraping HTML. Visitors also get a built-in chat assistant that answers questions from your docs.

<Tip>
  **See how your docs score.** Jamdesk scores your docs on every build. Open [AI Score](/ai/ai-score) in the dashboard for your grade and the issues to fix. The free [AI Score tool](https://jamdesk.com/ai-score) grades any documentation site on the open [AFDocs](https://afdocs.dev) standard. Grade sites as you browse with the [AI Score Chrome extension](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/jamdesk-ai-score/mhihkkgpcbmapmojnakhjfhjmiagkhlj).
</Tip>

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Write MDX">
    Create documentation pages in Markdown with optional React components.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Push and Build">
    Jamdesk builds your site and generates AI endpoints alongside your HTML.
  </Step>
  <Step title="AI Tools Connect">
    Claude, Cursor, ChatGPT, and other tools read your docs through llms.txt, MCP, or raw Markdown URLs.
  </Step>
</Steps>
